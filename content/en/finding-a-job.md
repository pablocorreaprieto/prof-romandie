## Compulsory schooling in brief

Primary level runs from year 1 to year 8 of schooling ([CDIP regulation](https://rsn.ne.ch/DATA/program/books/20223/pdf/416642.pdf)). Lower secondary follows, up to year 11. In the canton of Vaud, compulsory school takes pupils from age 4 to 15 ([Canton of Vaud](https://www.vd.ch/toutes-les-autorites/departements/departement-de-lenseignement-et-de-la-formation-professionnelle-def/direction-generale-de-lenseignement-obligatoire-et-de-la-pedagogie-specialisee-dgeo/)). Vaud writes the years 1P to 8P, Fribourg counts from 1H to 11H. The curriculum shared by all of French-speaking Switzerland is the Plan d'études romand, the PER.

## Vaud

The Directorate General for Compulsory Education (DGEO) is the employer, but school heads do the hiring. For a vacant post, you therefore contact them directly ([Canton of Vaud](https://www.vd.ch/formation/espace-enseignement/postuler)). The DGEO covers 94 compulsory-school establishments, grouped in eight regions ([Canton of Vaud](https://www.vd.ch/themes/formation/espace-enseignement/etre-une-ou-un-enseignant-en-activite)).

Jobs are posted on the canton's job portal. To apply, you need a recognised teaching qualification for primary or lower secondary.

Supply teaching goes through [MIREO](https://prestations.vd.ch/pub/mireo/), the registration platform for supply teachers in compulsory and special education ([Canton of Vaud](https://www.vd.ch/formation/espace-enseignement/faire-des-remplacements-dans-lenseignement)). A motion in the cantonal parliament sums it up. To do supply teaching, you only need to register on MIREO, and any adult can register ([cantonal parliament](https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/gc/fichiers_pdf/2022-2027/23_POS_44_depot.pdf)).

Your qualification changes your pay. Without a suitable teaching qualification, the salary drops by one class, and by two classes without any teaching qualification ([Canton of Vaud](https://www.vd.ch/etat-droit-finances/etat-employeur/salaire-et-assurances/reglements-de-mise-en-oeuvre/reduction-de-salaire-en-cas-dabsence-de-titre)). For the 2026 budget, the canton also lowered the rate for supply teachers without a teaching qualification ([cantonal government](https://www.vd.ch/actualites/decisions-du-conseil-detat/seance-du-conseil-detat/decision/id/9ca96ba3-7970-4c41-964c-c9bcb1c762a8)).

:::exp
I am registered on MIREO with my availability by region and school level. The site says that schools send in the periods worked at the end of the month and that pay arrives at the end of the following month. Several schools where I work also post their needs in WhatsApp groups. The posts are usually for the same day, the messages mostly arrive between 6:45 and 7:30 am, and the first person to answer often gets the job. A day of supply teaching earns me about 350 francs net. A day without supply work earns nothing.
:::

## Geneva

Geneva publishes precise hiring conditions for its generalist primary teachers ([Canton of Geneva](https://www.ge.ch/document/maitresses-maitres-generalistes)).

- A Geneva qualification or a bachelor's from a Swiss university of teacher education (HEP), a teaching diploma from an EU or EFTA state, or a qualification recognised by the CDIP.
- B2 certificates in German and English, and a C1 in French for some non-French-speaking candidates.
- A C, B or G permit valid on the hiring date.
- A criminal record extract and a certificate of good conduct less than three months old.

Primary supply teachers are managed by a central service, the SeREP. It only opens registration when it needs people, on dates that are not announced in advance, so check the page ge.ch/devenir-remplacant regularly ([Canton of Geneva](https://www.ge.ch/document/9453/telecharger)). At secondary level, supply teachers must hold the same diplomas as teachers, a university master's, or be studying for them ([Canton of Geneva](https://www.ge.ch/devenir-remplacante-remplacant/enseignement-secondaire-conditions-inscrire)).

## Valais

The education department keeps a pool of supply teachers. You enter your details and choices there, the department approves the registration, then you confirm every month that your data is correct and that you are still available ([Canton of Valais](https://ism-t.vs.ch/WebFile/Doc/Fr/ISM_RemplacementsEns.pdf)).

## Neuchâtel

Jobs for the school year are published in phases, in public job offers, with a job board on rpn.ch ([Canton of Neuchâtel](https://www.ne.ch/autorites/DESC/SRHE/offres-emploi/Documents/OPE_1erephase2026_document%20global.pdf)). A decree of 29 June 2026 asks supply teachers for an ordinary and a special criminal record extract. If they have lived in Switzerland for less than five years, they add an extract from their last country of residence ([Neuchâtel cantonal government](https://www.ne.ch/sites/default/files/2026-07/FO27_03_2026_06_29_DFFI_1102_ACE_Modification_RSten.pdf)).

## Fribourg

In the French-speaking part, compulsory school is run by the Service de l'enseignement obligatoire de langue française (SEnOF). For the 2026 school year, the canton advertised 358 posts in compulsory schooling, against 351 in 2025 ([Canton of Fribourg](https://www.fr.ch/document/602926)).

## Jura and the Bernese Jura

These regions train their teachers together with Neuchâtel, at the HEP-BEJUNE (Bern, Jura, Neuchâtel). For supply teaching, contact the education department of the canton concerned directly.

## Private and international schools

Everything above concerns state schools. Private schools, including the international schools around Geneva and Lausanne, hire on their own terms. The canton of Geneva says that for a foreign diploma, a private employer will judge case by case ([Canton of Geneva](https://www.ge.ch/node/9478)). For an English-speaking teacher without C2 French, that is, in my view, the most realistic way in, and it can sit alongside a CDIP application. I have not worked in one, so I do not describe their pay or contracts here.

## How I go about it

What follows is my opinion, not a rule.

- At first, be available for one-day supply jobs. It is the fastest way to get known by schools.
- Write to school heads rather than waiting for an advert.
- Then aim for long replacements. I did two in 2026, at secondary level and then in 4P.
- Keep a starter kit ready for each new class.

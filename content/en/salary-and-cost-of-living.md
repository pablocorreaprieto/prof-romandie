- The canton of Vaud pays on a scale of ==18 classes==, each split into 26 steps, 13th month included.
- In 2025, class 9 runs from ==CHF&nbsp;77,068 to 111,749== a year and class 11 from ==CHF&nbsp;90,617 to 131,395==.
- Without a suitable teaching qualification, one class less. Without any teaching qualification, two classes less.
- Without a C permit, tax is in principle deducted at source, on your payslip.
:::

## The Vaud pay scale

In the canton of Vaud, each job corresponds to a level, and each level to a salary class. The scale has 18 classes of 26 steps. You normally move up one step a year until the top of the class, if the employment has lasted at least six months ([Canton of Vaud](https://www.vd.ch/etat-droit-finances/etat-employeur/le-systeme-salarial)). Recognised experience sets your starting step ([RSRC](https://www.lexfind.ch/tol/24198/de)).

| Class | Minimum per year | Maximum per year |
|---|---|---|
| 9 | CHF&nbsp;77,068 | CHF&nbsp;111,749 |
| 10 | CHF&nbsp;83,450 | CHF&nbsp;121,002 |
| 11 | CHF&nbsp;90,617 | CHF&nbsp;131,395 |

Canton of Vaud 2025 scale, gross annual salaries at 100%, 13th month included ([RSRC, annex 2](https://www.lexfind.ch/tol/24198/de)).

Which class for a teacher? When the current system came in, at the end of 2008, generalist teachers were placed at level 9 and teachers of academic subjects at level 11. That is what judgments of the cantonal employment tribunal report ([judgment of 17 June 2016](https://prestations.vd.ch/pub/101623/api/decision/download/665eaa40-faa5-499b-9b3d-debffb2c9614)). Special measures have been taken since for some jobs, so rely on the class written in your contract.

The cantonal government gives a worked example. A teacher of academic subjects in class 11 went from CHF&nbsp;102,048 in 2020, at step 7, to CHF&nbsp;114,330 in 2025, at step 12 ([cantonal parliament](https://www.vd.ch/gc/seances-du-grand-conseil/point-seance/point/7915ef78-2839-4019-8b7c-ed2057bcf715/meeting/1029648)).

## Without recognition, one or two classes less

The Vaud rule fits in two lines. Without a suitable teaching qualification, the salary is reduced by one class. Without any teaching qualification, it is reduced by two classes ([Canton of Vaud](https://www.vd.ch/etat-droit-finances/etat-employeur/salaire-et-assurances/reglements-de-mise-en-oeuvre/reduction-de-salaire-en-cas-dabsence-de-titre)). For the 2026 budget, the canton lowered the rate for supply teachers without a teaching qualification again ([cantonal government](https://www.vd.ch/actualites/decisions-du-conseil-detat/seance-du-conseil-detat/decision/id/9ca96ba3-7970-4c41-964c-c9bcb1c762a8)). That is a very concrete reason to apply to the CDIP as early as possible.

## What leaves before it reaches your account

**Tax at source.** In the canton of Vaud, a foreign employee without a C permit is in principle taxed at source, directly on their salary. Above CHF&nbsp;120,000 gross annual income, a subsequent ordinary assessment applies automatically ([Canton of Vaud](https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/annexe_Erratum.pdf)). Below that, you can request one in writing until 31 March of the following year. Once filed, the request cannot be withdrawn ([Canton of Vaud](https://www.vd.ch/etat-droit-finances/impots/pour-les-employeurs/impot-a-la-source/personnes-imposees-a-la-source-/-sourciers)).

**Health insurance.** It is compulsory and individual. You have three months after arriving to take it out ([FOPH](https://www.bag.admin.ch/fr/assurance-maladie-obligation-de-sassurer-pour-les-assures-domicilies-en-suisse)). The premium depends on the insurer, the deductible and the region. In 2026, premiums rise by 4.9% on average in the canton of Vaud ([Canton of Vaud](https://www.vd.ch/typo3temp/assets/pdfs/les-primes-dassurance-maladie-vont-augmenter-de-49-en-moyenne-en-2026.pdf)). The federal government publishes an official comparison site, priminfo.admin.ch.

**Social contributions.** They are deducted from your salary, in particular old-age insurance (AVS) and the occupational pension. The exact amounts are on your payslip.

## What a supply teacher earns

:::exp
A day of supply teaching earns me about 350 francs net. Days without supply work earn nothing, and a month's pay arrives at the end of the following month.
:::

For a first budget, my advice is to start from the net pay for one day multiplied by a realistic number of days, not from the annual salary on a scale.

## What I leave out

I give neither average rents nor a model budget. They depend too much on the commune and go out of date quickly. I have only included the Vaud scale, the one I know and could check. The other cantons have their own scales, published by their administrations.

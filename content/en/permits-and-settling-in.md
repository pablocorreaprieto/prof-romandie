:::bref
- EU or EFTA citizen with a contract of at least a year or open-ended, ==B permit== valid five years.
- Contract of three months to a year, ==L permit== for the length of the contract.
- UK citizens arriving since 2021 come under a separate quota, ==2,100 B and 1,400 L permits== for 2026.
- Register with the commune within ==14 days== of arriving, before starting work.
:::

## EU and EFTA citizens

The agreement on the free movement of persons gives you the right to a permit if you have an employment contract. Its type depends on the length of the contract ([SEM](https://www.sem.admin.ch/sem/fr/home/themen/fza_schweiz-eu-efta/meldeverfahren/ref_faq.html)).

- Up to three months per calendar year, no permit, but an online notification.
- From three months to a year, an L permit, valid for the length of the contract ([SEM](https://www.sem.admin.ch/sem/fr/home/themen/aufenthalt/eu_efta/ausweis_l_eu_efta.html)).
- A year or more, or an open-ended contract, a B permit valid five years ([Canton of Vaud](https://www.vd.ch/population/population-etrangere/entree-et-sejour/union-europeenne/prise-dune-activite-salariee-par-un-ressortissant-dun-pays-ue-27/aele)).

Within 14 days of arriving, and before you start work, you must register with the commune where you want to live to apply for your residence permit ([SEM](https://www.sem.admin.ch/sem/fr/home/themen/fza_schweiz-eu-efta/meldeverfahren/ref_faq.html)).

## Living across the border

If you live in an EU or EFTA state and work in Switzerland, you need a G permit, the cross-border commuter permit. EU and EFTA citizens are no longer tied to border zones. They can live in any EU or EFTA state and work anywhere in Switzerland ([SEM](https://www.sem.admin.ch/fr/faq-libre-circulation-des-personnes)). The condition is to return to your main home at least once a week ([EPFL](https://epfl.ch/campus/services/human-resources/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Permis-en-bref-2023.pdf)). Geneva accepts the G permit for its generalist primary teachers ([Canton of Geneva](https://www.ge.ch/document/maitresses-maitres-generalistes)).

## UK citizens

Since 1 January 2021, UK citizens who move to Switzerland are no longer covered by free movement. They are treated as third-country nationals, and their employer must first apply for a work permit from the canton ([SEM](https://www.sem.admin.ch/en/brexit-en)). They have a quota of their own. For 2026, the Federal Council set it at 3,500 permits for people in employment, 2,100 B permits and 1,400 L permits ([SEM](https://www.sem.admin.ch/en/newnsb/7HwBjdg5HpBA)). The same press release notes that these separate UK quotas were introduced as a transitional measure.

UK citizens who already had rights under free movement up to 31 December 2020 keep them under a Swiss-UK agreement ([SEM](https://www.sem.admin.ch/en/brexit-en)). If that is your case, check your situation with your canton's population office.

## Outside the EU and EFTA

For other nationalities, the rules are much stricter. Only qualified people can be admitted, and the employer must show that it found no one on the Swiss market or in the EU or EFTA ([SEM](https://www.sem.admin.ch/sem/fr/home/themen/arbeit/nicht-eu_efta-angehoerige.html)). For 2026, the quota for these workers is 8,500 permits, 4,500 B and 4,000 L ([SEM](https://www.sem.admin.ch/en/newnsb/7HwBjdg5HpBA)). For a job in a state school, I think that is a very high barrier. On the CDIP side, the fee is higher (CHF&nbsp;1000) and the processing time can be considerably longer ([CDIP](https://edudoc.ch/record/209282/files/mb_allgemein_ausl_lehrdipl_f.pdf)).

## The first weeks, in order

1. **Before you leave.** Keep your diplomas with you, the CDIP decision or proof of your application, and recent criminal record extracts. Geneva asks for them on hiring, Neuchâtel from supply teachers.
2. **On arrival.** Register with the commune within 14 days, before your first day of work.
3. **Within three months.** Take out health insurance. Within that period, it covers you from the day you arrived ([FOPH](https://www.bag.admin.ch/fr/assurance-maladie-obligation-de-sassurer-pour-les-assures-domicilies-en-suisse)).
4. **At your first pay.** Look at the tax withheld at source and keep your payslips. You will need them if you request a subsequent ordinary assessment before 31 March of the following year ([Canton of Vaud](https://www.vd.ch/etat-droit-finances/impots/pour-les-employeurs/impot-a-la-source/personnes-imposees-a-la-source-/-sourciers)).

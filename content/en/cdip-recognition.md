:::bref
- The CDIP, in Bern, examines teaching diplomas obtained abroad. Its decision is valid in every canton.
- The fee is ==CHF&nbsp;800== for a diploma from the EU or EFTA and ==CHF&nbsp;1000== for any other country, according to the 2022 fact sheet.
- For an EU diploma, the review takes ==four months at most== once the file is complete.
- Recognition does not expire, but it does not entitle you to a job.
:::

## Who decides

In Switzerland, each canton runs its own schools. Recognition of foreign teaching diplomas, however, is handled by a single body, the Swiss Conference of Cantonal Ministers of Education (CDIP, called EDK in German). It compares your training with the matching Swiss diploma and issues a decision that is valid in every canton, with no time limit ([CDIP fact sheet](https://edudoc.ch/record/209282/files/mb_allgemein_ausl_lehrdipl_f.pdf)).

The canton of Vaud says so plainly on its recruitment page. To apply, you need a recognised teaching qualification for primary or lower secondary, and a foreign diploma goes through an equivalence request to the CDIP. The page adds that this can take a while and should be started early ([Canton of Vaud](https://www.vd.ch/formation/espace-enseignement/postuler)).

## The steps of the application

1. You submit your application online, on the CDIP portal.
2. The CDIP asks you to pay the fee.
3. The secretariat checks that the file is complete. If a document is missing, it asks for it, and the application waits until you send it.
4. A panel of experts compares your training with the Swiss training.
5. You receive the decision.

The document checklist and the link to the portal are on the [CDIP's foreign diplomas page](https://www.cdip.ch/fr/themes/reconnaissance-des-diplomes/diplomes-etrangers). The procedure runs in French, German or Italian, not in English.

## What it costs

The fact sheet of 29 November 2022 sets the fee at ==CHF&nbsp;800== for a diploma from an EU or EFTA state and ==CHF&nbsp;1000== for a diploma from any other country. It does not name the United Kingdom, which left the EU in 2020, so check which fee the portal shows for a UK diploma. The document is a few years old, so check the amount on the day you apply anyway.

Other costs are yours too. There are translations and certified copies of your documents, then the cost of any compensation measures, billed directly by the institution that runs them.

## How long it takes

For a diploma from an EU state, the review takes ==four months at most== from the moment the complete file reaches the CDIP secretariat. For other countries, the CDIP warns that it can take considerably longer.

The word that matters is "complete". As long as a document is missing, the clock does not start.

:::exp
I applied on 24 June 2025, with a Spanish primary teaching diploma (with a specialisation in French as a foreign language). The decision is dated 28 May 2026. Eleven months in all, for an official four months that only start once the file is complete.
:::

## What the CDIP can decide

- **The trainings are equivalent.** You receive recognition.
- **They are comparable, with significant differences.** The CDIP asks for compensation measures, usually an adaptation period in a Swiss teacher-training institution. Work experience or further training can be enough to cover the gap.
- **The gaps are too large.** The application is refused.

## Reading your decision

A decision states a school level and a list of subjects. Mine declares my diploma equivalent to a Swiss diploma for primary level, from year 3 to year 8. The list includes French, English and maths, but not German.

That detail matters in French-speaking Switzerland, where German is taught from year 5 and English from year 7 ([CIIP](https://edudoc.ch/record/104591/files/cycle_2_webCIIP.pdf)). Cantons can also set their own hiring conditions. Geneva, for example, asks its generalist primary teachers for B2 certificates in German and English ([Canton of Geneva](https://www.ge.ch/document/maitresses-maitres-generalistes)).

Keep in mind what the CDIP points out. Recognition concerns your qualification. It does not give you a right to any particular job.

## And the language

To teach French, the CDIP generally asks for a C2 language diploma. If your diploma is from the EU, EFTA or the United Kingdom, you can provide it during the procedure, within two years of applying ([language fact sheet](https://edudoc.ch/record/216238/files/aide-memoire-langue-f.pdf)). Everything is explained in [step 2](@francais).

## Before you apply

:::check en-cdip
- Gather your diploma and your transcript or diploma supplement
- Compare your documents with the CDIP's official checklist
- Check which documents must be translated, and by whom
- Prepare your C2 language diploma, or note the two-year deadline
- Keep a PDF copy of everything you send
:::

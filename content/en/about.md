My name is Pablo Correa Prieto. I trained as a primary teacher at the University of La Laguna, in the Canary Islands, with a specialisation in French as a foreign language. In May 2026, the CDIP recognised my diploma for primary level, from year 3 to year 8. I work as a supply teacher in the canton of Vaud and I am doing a master's in education research.

I wrote this guide because the official information exists, but it is spread between Bern, the cantons and the countries teachers come from. Here it is put back in the order of the steps, with a link to every source.

## How I work

- I only cite official documents I have opened, with the date I checked them.
- I keep what the texts say apart from what I went through. My own experience is always marked as such.
- I do not give legal advice. For a specific case, the competent authority has the final word.
- I correct quickly when someone points out a mistake. Write to me at {{contact}}.
